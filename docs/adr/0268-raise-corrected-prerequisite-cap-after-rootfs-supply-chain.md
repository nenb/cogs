# ADR 0268: Raise the corrected prerequisite cap after rootfs supply-chain implementation

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-31
- Accepted by: Nick Byrne through the standing instruction to complete the corrected non-AWS sequence autonomously and stop before AWS effects

## Context

ADR 0267 allocated readable capacity before implementation. The now-tested rootfs supply-chain slice adds the shared producer-independent plan model, closed descriptor/schema, strict GHCR acquisition, one-import lease path, durable descriptor lineage, V3 static control, V4 local evidence bindings, a two-build candidate producer, and a separate directional signed publisher. Portable hostile, acquisition, immutable-preparation, materializer, ledger, lease, static-control, local-result, and receipt tests pass.

Measured no-deletion-credit additions are now 15,854 deployment, 6,560 retained schema/script, 1,864 workflow, and 24,278 global. Current physical total is 76,453 and conservative total is 79,632. ADR 0267 leaves only 1,646 deployment, 940 retained, 436 workflow, and 1,722 global lines, which cannot readably hold the already-mapped production cycle capability, controller custody, final inventory, duration, AMI/artifact binding, evidence publisher, workflow conversion, and supersession package.

## Decision

Raise correction highs to 19,500 deployment, 9,500 retained schema/script, 3,000 workflow, and 32,000 global. Raise the advisory preferred total to 90,000 and hard stop to 94,000. Raise the tracked-source cardinality bound from 1,250 to 1,300.

These remain non-transferable maxima, not targets. Deletion, movement, replacement, compression, generation, omission, or historical-file mutation creates no credit. New capacity is limited to the machine-mapped ADR 0267 prerequisites, readable hostile tests, additive contracts, exact producer/consumer/controller seams, and final supersession evidence. Kata, SSH, network, workload, teardown, and AWS effect logic remain out of scope except for a demonstrated narrow binding requirement.

No formal qualification is authorized by this cap. H/G still cannot freeze until the complete non-AWS graph, no-mint KVM rehearsal, and clean whole-graph review pass. No AWS credential, provider/OpenTofu effect, SSM command, inventory query, deployment, campaign, or product release is authorized.
