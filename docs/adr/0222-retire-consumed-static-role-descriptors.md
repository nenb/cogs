# ADR 0222: Retire consumed static-role descriptors

- Status: Accepted under the owner's explicit standing authorization for all non-AWS prerequisite work
- Date: 2026-08-28

H66 still reported only runtime-marked descriptors after the duplicated executable owner was retired. Static admission retained the original descriptors behind each consumed executable-role custody claim; the executable owner held and closed separate duplicates. Those originals therefore remained until whole static-custody closure after independent residue observation.

After every executable claim and duplicate owner has been exactly retired, require all reviewed executable roles to have one consumed static claim. Remove only those unique original role-object descriptors from static custody, remove their consumed claims, and close them exactly. The remaining control, source, mapping, prepared-runtime, and receipt custody is preserved for final evidence. Any missing role, unconsumed claim, duplicate descriptor, foreign descriptor, outstanding duplicate claim, or close error fails closed. Whole-custody abort can no longer double-close retired role descriptors.

Focused admission, mutable-bridge, coordinator, TypeScript, formatting, and retained-line matrices pass. H66 remains a private non-authoritative diagnostic and minted no qualification.

This grants no AWS, provider, deployment, campaign, production, release, or qualification authority.
